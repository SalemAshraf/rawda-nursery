# Nursery feature priorities, November 22, 2026

The strongest next product investment is a permissioned parent experience, followed by fee automation and lower-friction care entry. Admissions/waitlists, internal daily care, pickup permissions, manual fees and management reporting are already present in this upgrade. The landing and sales platform describe those implemented capabilities, not the entire competitor feature set.

## Ranked priorities

| Rank | Capability | Evidence and fit | Dependency or risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parent self-service and scoped daily communication | Hadana, Parent GCC, iCare Egypt, Famly and Brightwheel put family communication alongside nursery operations. Famly documents the actual parent view, rather than only a marketing promise. | Verified guardian identity, per-child access, consent, incident acknowledgement attribution, moderated media and delivery infrastructure. A staff-recorded acknowledgement is not a parent login. |
| 2 | Recurring invoice preparation, receipts and exports | Hadana and Brightwheel describe recurring billing/reminders and financial reports. Parent GCC stresses consistent branch billing and payment visibility. | Preserve manual approval, duplicates protection, exact currency precision, exports and regional payment/tax research before activating collection. |
| 3 | Room-level care entry and planned absences | Hadana lists room-wide logging, planned absence and unexplained absence follow-up; Brightwheel documents absence and attendance reports. | Never overwrite individual care notes or imply that an absent child received care. Keep allergy and attendance context visible. |
| 4 | Documents, permissions and learning observations | Hadana and Brightwheel describe document requests, admissions and development records. Famly exposes permission tasks and activity plans to parents. | Private file storage, retention/consent choices and educator-led review. Do not market compliance or curriculum certification without verification. |
| 5 | Staff schedules and multi-branch operations | Parent GCC and Hadana list scheduling/branch support. | Tenant/branch permission design and actual staffing policies; raw capacity is not a legal ratio. |

## Criteria and assumptions

Egypt/Gulf relevance, repeated evidence across vendors, fit with the current internal nursery workflow, impact on staff/parent trust and dependency cost. Vendor pages establish what those vendors claim, not independent proof of implementation quality. No competitor price is copied into the product. Regional tax, payment, retention and licensing rules remain separate checks. No revenue, time-saving, security-certification or adoption claims are inferred.
